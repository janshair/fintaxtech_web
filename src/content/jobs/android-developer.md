## About the role

FinTaxTech builds native Android apps, including tools for invoicing and workout tracking. We are looking for an Android Developer who can take a feature from acceptance criteria through implementation, testing and Google Play release preparation.

## What you will work on

- Build accessible, adaptive screens with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and Material 3.
- Implement feature state, navigation and business rules in a modular Gradle project.
- Build offline-first data flows with Room and DataStore, and integrate REST APIs when the app needs them.
- Test business rules, persistence and key user journeys with unit and Compose UI tests.
- Integrate crash reporting, analytics or subscriptions where they are part of the product.
- Prepare signed builds, store screenshots and Google Play submission materials.

## You will be working on

- Kotlin, coroutines and Flow.
- Jetpack Compose, Material 3 and state-driven UI.
- Room, schema migrations and local persistence.
- Gradle, Android Studio, Git and automated testing.
- Experience taking a native Android feature through implementation and release preparation.

Familiarity with Hilt, Firebase, RevenueCat or Google Play Billing would be useful. Experience with adaptive layouts, right-to-left localisation or GitHub Actions is also welcome.

## How we approach the work

Keep changes small and reviewable. Verify that screens handle empty, loading and error states, and check data migrations and core journeys before preparing a release.
