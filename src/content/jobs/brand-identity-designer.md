## About the role

FinTaxTech designs visual identities for small businesses and creators. We are looking for a Brand Identity Designer who can turn a client brief into a visual system and carry it through to the materials the client uses, from a website header to a business card or a workwear design.

## What you will work on

- Review client briefs and references, develop logo concepts and explain the decisions behind each direction.
- Refine approved concepts into a logo family with suitable layouts and colour variants.
- Choose typography and colour palettes and document their use in practical brand guidelines.
- Apply the identity to stationery, flyers, workwear artwork, social profiles and reusable graphics as agreed for each project.
- Prepare editable SVG and layered artwork alongside the approved print and digital exports.
- Proof artwork for accurate copy, legibility, print specifications and platform safe areas before handover.

## You will be working on

- A portfolio showing logo or visual identity work applied across more than one format.
- Typography, colour, layout and vector drawing.
- Vector and layered design tools and the ability to prepare print-ready files and platform-specific exports.
- Clear presentation of design decisions, careful proofing and organised source files.

Experience designing apparel artwork, reusable social templates or video-call backgrounds would be useful. Familiarity with refining AI-assisted concepts by hand is also welcome.

## How we approach the work

Agree the brief and deliverables up front, present concepts with a clear rationale and incorporate feedback in review rounds. Check the approved design at the sizes and in the formats the client will actually use.
